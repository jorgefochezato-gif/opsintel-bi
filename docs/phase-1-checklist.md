## Completion Criteria

Phase 1 is complete when:

1. The repository foundation is established.
2. The business scenario is documented.
3. Initial KPIs are defined.
4. The architecture is documented.
5. Initial analytical business processes are identified.
6. Candidate fact-table grains are documented.
7. All Phase 1 work has been reviewed through a pull request.
8. The feature branch is merged into `main`.
9. Local and remote `main` are synchronized.
